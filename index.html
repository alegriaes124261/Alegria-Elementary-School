About Our School

Alegria Elementary School, Division of Zamboanga del Norte, Baliguian District is committed to provide quality, inclusive, safe, and learner-centered education. We work together with parents, teachers, learners, and community partners to help every child grow academically, socially, emotionally, and morally upright.
